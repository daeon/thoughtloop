# Discover

One discovery engine supports three independent modes:

- `solutions`: search materially different solution or causal families without selecting the winner;
- `challenge`: test inherited constraints, problem framing, and assumptions;
- `prototype`: make cheap observable options, mockups, references, or probes.

Inspect the objective, hard requirements, evidence, budget, and decision that could change. Prefer structural differences in boundary, mechanism, state, timing, operational burden, risk, or cost. Stop when relevant families are represented or measurement is more valuable than more ideas.

Return an `OptionSet` or `DiscoveryBrief` with assumptions, tradeoffs, tests, and unresolved questions. Do not implement production behavior, rank options, or force a quota of alternatives. A graph may pass the result to `decide`; an independent call remains useful on its own.
