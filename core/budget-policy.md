# Budget and delegation policy

ThoughtLoop owns delegation. Canonical nodes may recommend a narrow opportunity,
but they do not spawn orchestrators or recursively delegate.

| Budget | Starting shape |
|---|---|
| light | one narrow independent question |
| balanced | up to two complementary questions |
| deep | up to three complementary questions and one follow-up round |

Use fresh context unless inherited context is necessary (fork_context=false or
the platform equivalent). The parent owns the task contract, synthesis, edits,
final verdict, and user communication. Across all budgets, allow at most one
follow-up delegation round.

Use lower-cost agents for bounded inspection, search, or mechanical review when
adequate; reserve stronger reasoning for disputed evidence, difficult tradeoffs,
and final synthesis.
